This site collects anonymous, aggregate statistics about how it is used. It does not identify visitors, it sets no cookies, and there is no cookie banner because there is nothing to ask consent for.

What is collected

Visitor statistics are provided by Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless analytics service. For each page view it records:

  • the page that was viewed, and the referring domain (not the full referring URL);
  • an approximate country, derived from the request;
  • device type, browser and operating system;
  • the time of the visit.

What is not collected

  • No analytics cookies or analytics identifiers in local storage. The site’s appearance settings are stored locally as described below.
  • No cross-site or cross-device tracking, and no attempt to recognise you on a later visit.
  • No fingerprinting, no profiling, no advertising, no data sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No accounts, no forms, no newsletter — there is nothing on this site that asks for personal information.

Appearance preferences on your device

The site reads and saves theme and taiji-rot in your browser’s local storage to remember your light or dark theme and the theme button’s orientation. These values are used only for the interface and are not sent to the analytics service. If your browser blocks local storage, you can still change the theme for the current page.

Who processes it

Cloudflare, Inc. provides the analytics service and processes the request data on my behalf as a service provider. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for how they handle it. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for the request logs that hosting involves.

How long it is kept

Aggregate statistics are retained for no longer than 13 months and are not renewed automatically by new visits.

How to object

The locally saved appearance preferences do not identify you or track your visits. If you would still prefer not to be counted in the aggregate statistics, either:

  • enable Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser, or
  • email me at zili.tan@student.uva.nl and I will exclude your visits.

Questions about any of the above are welcome at the same address. This page was last updated on 2 October 2026.
