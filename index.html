Ongoing Research Projects

MemTurn: Continual Learning in Resource-Constrained Agents

Exploratory research · In progress

I built MemTurn to study which experiences an agent should keep so it can continue learning as new tasks arrive, even with limited memory and compute. To track overall performance, I defined cumulative utility (UH) as the sum of scores on tasks completed on time. I designed two metrics to assess the value of prior experience under the same feedback budget. Gold measures how much that experience helps an agent handle familiar types of tasks. Gnew measures how much it helps the agent learn new types of tasks.

MemTurn (GitHub)

Agentic AI Workflows for SMEFT Event Generation at the LHC

MSc thesis · In progress · Advisor: Prof. Wouter Verkerke, Nikhef / University of Amsterdam

I built a MadGraph workflow for parton-level event generation. Ablation experiments showed that validation gates improved its task success rate. Using ATLAS Monte Carlo job options, I designed a 17-task benchmark on which the workflow achieved a 94.1% success rate with a simulated user.

Future Research Plans

Revising Assumptions During Reasoning

In theoretical physics, a derivation can lead to an unphysical solution. We check the steps and ask whether an assumption has been pushed beyond its range of validity. Any revision still has to fit the evidence and respect known physical principles. This leads me to a question I would like to study through reinforcement learning. When an agent’s reasoning stalls, can it learn when to pursue its current line of reasoning, when to seek more evidence, and when it has grounds to revise a particular assumption? Can that judgment transfer to unfamiliar tasks?
