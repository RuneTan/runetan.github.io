Research Experience

Observer-Relative Inflationary Histories in de Sitter JT Gravity Advisors: Prof. Ben Freivogel and Prof. Jan Pieter van der Schaar, University of Amsterdam, 06/2026 – present.

  • Studied de Sitter JT gravity coupled to a homogeneous inflaton and a weakly coupled observer, using the observer-relative gravitational path integral (GPI).
  • Computed the no-boundary amplitude, gravitational and inflaton one-loop determinants, and the global de Sitter and wormhole contributions to the observer-relative no-boundary probability.
  • Found that the current observer-relative GPI prescription does not normalize the no-boundary state, indicating a possible inconsistency of the Hartle-Hawking proposal in the observer-relative Hilbert space.

Holographic Cosmology Advisor: Prof. Chengcheng Han, Sun Yat-sen University, 09/2024 – 04/2025.

  • Studied the holographic principle, holographic entropy bounds, and holographic cosmology models, focusing on the Banks–Fischler framework built on cosmological duality.
  • Analyzed how holographic models address the low-entropy initial condition problem and produce a nearly scale-invariant primordial power spectrum, including quantitative corrections and non-Gaussianity.
  • Evaluated the prospects for testing holographic cosmology with future CMB and primordial gravitational-wave observations.

Inflationary Cosmology Advisor: Prof. Chengcheng Han, Sun Yat-sen University, 10/2022 – 12/2023.

  • Studied the standard cosmological model and inflationary cosmology, reviewed slow-roll models, and compared their predicted power spectra with Planck TT observations.
  • Authored a final report rated Excellent and received a university-level award (03/2024) in the College Students Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program.
